Important: ChatingFly is designed as a browser-based peer-to-peer communication service. Please read this policy before using messaging, file-sharing, or video-call features.

1. About ChatingFly

ChatingFly (“ChatingFly”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is a browser-based communication service operated by y@$h.

ChatingFly may provide peer-to-peer text messaging, file and image sharing, and browser-based audio/video communication.

2. Information You Provide

ChatingFly may allow you to use the service without creating a traditional account.

Depending on the features you use, you may provide:

  • A user-selected ID or nickname.
  • A friend's user ID.
  • Messages that you choose to send.
  • Files or images that you choose to transfer.
  • Camera and microphone access when you choose to use video calls.

Do not intentionally send passwords, payment information, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information through ChatingFly.

3. Peer-to-Peer Communication

ChatingFly is designed to use browser technologies such as WebRTC and PeerJS for peer-to-peer communication.

When a connection is successfully established, communication data may be transferred directly between participating browsers, depending on network conditions and the underlying WebRTC connection.

Peer-to-peer communication does not mean that every part of the connection process is completely server-free. Signaling and network traversal services may be involved in establishing connections.

Therefore, ChatingFly does not promise that the service is “100% anonymous”, “100% serverless”, or absolutely private.

4. Messages

ChatingFly is designed primarily for browser-to-browser communication. The current application is not intended to provide a permanent, public message-storage system.

Messages may disappear when a browser session or peer connection ends, depending on the implementation and connection state.

You should not assume that a message is permanently private or unrecoverable. A person you communicate with may copy, save, photograph, record, or otherwise retain information you send.

5. File and Image Sharing

ChatingFly may allow users to transfer files or images to another connected user.

Users are responsible for making sure that they have the legal right to share the files they transmit.

ChatingFly does not guarantee that files received from another user are safe, accurate, lawful, or free from malicious software.

Use appropriate caution before opening files received from other users.

6. Camera and Microphone

ChatingFly's video-call feature may request access to your device's camera and microphone.

Your browser and device control these permissions. ChatingFly requests them when you choose to start or accept a video call.

You may deny or revoke camera and microphone permissions through your browser or device settings.

ChatingFly may use third-party technologies and infrastructure to operate its features.

These services may process technical information required to provide their respective functions.

Third-party services may have their own terms and privacy policies.
