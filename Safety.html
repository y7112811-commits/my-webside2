Safety & Community Guidelines · by y@$h

Guidelines designed to help keep ChatingFly respectful, responsible and safer for everyone.

1. Use ChatingFly Responsibly

ChatingFly is a browser-based communication platform that may provide peer-to-peer messaging, file sharing and video communication. Users are responsible for how they use these features and for the content they transmit.

Good practices

  • Communicate respectfully.
  • Only share content that you have the right to share.
  • Only communicate with people you know or trust.
  • Keep your personal information private.
  • End a conversation or connection if you feel uncomfortable.

2. Do Not Use ChatingFly For Abuse

Users should not use ChatingFly to threaten, harass, intimidate, impersonate, scam, or deliberately harm another person.

Prohibited behavior includes

  • Threats or targeted harassment.
  • Fraud, scams or deceptive activity.
  • Impersonating another person.
  • Spam or deliberate disruption of the service.
  • Sharing another person's private information without permission.
  • Using the service for unlawful activity.

3. File Sharing Safety

ChatingFly may allow users to send files directly between browsers. Do not open files from people you do not trust.

  • Check the file before opening it.
  • Do not share passwords, private documents or sensitive information unnecessarily.
  • Only share files you are legally allowed to distribute.
  • Do not use file sharing to distribute harmful or unlawful material.

4. Video Call Safety

Video and microphone features require browser permissions. Before granting camera or microphone access, make sure you understand which website you are using and who you are communicating with.

  • Do not share private information during calls.
  • End the call if you feel unsafe or uncomfortable.
  • Do not record or redistribute another person's private call without appropriate permission or legal authority.

5. Protect Your Personal Information

Avoid sharing information such as your home address, passwords, financial information, private documents, authentication codes or other sensitive information through chat.

Remember

A username, room code or peer ID should not be treated as a password or security guarantee. Only share connection information with people you intend to communicate with.

6. Peer-to-Peer Communication

ChatingFly may use WebRTC and PeerJS to establish browser-to-browser communication. The technical behavior of browsers, WebRTC, networking infrastructure and third-party services can vary.

Users should not assume that a P2P connection makes every type of information completely anonymous or guarantees complete security.

7. Report a Safety or Abuse Concern

If you believe ChatingFly is being misused or you have a privacy, security or safety concern, contact:

ChatingFly Support
Email: chatingfly@gmail.com

When reporting an issue, provide enough information for the ChatingFly team to understand the problem. Do not send passwords, authentication codes or unnecessary private information.

8. Platform Limitations

These guidelines are intended to encourage responsible use. They do not guarantee that harmful, illegal or abusive activity can always be prevented or detected.

ChatingFly may update its features, technical architecture and safety practices over time.
